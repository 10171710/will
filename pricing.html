On fees

Questions about money

Ask us anything else before you commit to a rupee.

Ask a fee question
Could the fixed fee ever change?‎

Only if the facts change materially — you tell us about a business we did not know about, or a second property abroad. In that case we re-quote in writing before doing any further work, and you are free to stop there.

When do I pay?‎

Nothing at the first consultation. Planning work is payable on signing, or in three monthly instalments at no extra cost. Litigation and probate work is billed in agreed stages so you always know what is next.

What are court fees, and who pays them?‎

They are statutory charges calculated as a percentage of the value certified or of the estate. They are paid to the court, not to us, and are usually met from the estate. We compute them and show the receipt.

Is the first consultation genuinely free?‎

Yes. Forty-five minutes, no charge, no obligation, and we will tell you if you do not need us yet. Roughly one visitor in six leaves without instructing us, which is exactly as it should be.

Do you accept cards and bank transfer?‎

Bank transfer, UPI and card. Every payment is receipted to our client account, and you receive a GST invoice the same day.
