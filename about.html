Mission & values

What we hold ourselves to

These are not decorations for a website. They are the four things we check ourselves against when a matter gets difficult.

Plain language

If you cannot explain a clause back to us in your own words, we have not finished writing it.

Absolute discretion

Nothing leaves this office without your written instruction — including that you were ever here.

Fixed fees

Agreed in writing before we start, not revisited because something took longer than expected.

No hurrying

People make poor decisions under pressure. We would rather you took another week to think.

Our history

Twenty-six years, quietly

We have grown slowly and on purpose. Every partner still carries their own files.

  1. 00

    2000

    A single room on Richmond Row

    Anita Willbridge leaves a large commercial firm to draft wills properly, for a fixed fee, in language people can read. Two clients in the first month.

  2. 07

    2007

    Probate practice opens

    Too many clients were coming back as executors with nowhere to turn. We built a dedicated team so the drafting lawyer's family could ring the same number afterwards.

  3. 14

    2014

    Trusts and special needs planning

    A parent asked how to provide for a son who would never manage money. The answer took months to build, and became a practice area of its own.

  4. 21

    2021

    Remote consultations, permanently

    What began as a necessity turned out to suit families spread across three time zones. Signings remain resolutely in person.

  5. 26

    2026

    Four thousand families later

    Eleven people, one office, the same fixed-fee promise. We have turned down two acquisition offers to keep it that way.
