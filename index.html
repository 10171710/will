Common questions

Things people ask before they call

There are no silly questions here — only ones nobody has answered for you yet.

Read the full FAQ
Do I really need a will if my family already knows my wishes?‎

Knowing your wishes and being able to act on them are different things. Banks, registrars and courts need a document. Without one, your family must ask a judge for permission to do what you had already decided.

How long does a straightforward will take?‎

Usually two appointments over about three weeks — one to understand your situation, one to sign. Complex estates with businesses or overseas assets take longer, and we will say so up front.

Is the first consultation really free?‎

Yes — 45 minutes, with no obligation. If we think you do not need us yet, we will tell you that too. Nothing is billed before you approve a written fixed fee.

Will anything I say be shared with my family?‎

Nothing at all — including the fact that you came to see us. Your file is released only on your written instruction, or to your executor after death.
