Quick answers

About the online service

Is an online will legally valid?‎

Yes — validity comes from correct drafting, capacity and proper witnessed signing, not from where it was typed. That is exactly why we insist on a lawyer review and an in-person signing rather than selling you a download.

What if my situation is complicated?‎

The questionnaire flags it, the review lawyer confirms it, and we move you to full advisory — crediting everything you have already paid. Business shares, agricultural land, overseas assets and trusts all need a conversation.

Do you store the original?‎

If you want us to, at no extra cost, in our strongroom with your executor notified. Otherwise you take it home and we keep only the encrypted copy in your account.

Can I change it later?‎

Free amendments for three years, then ₹2,500 per revision. Substantial changes are done as a fresh will that revokes the old one, and we handle the re-signing.
